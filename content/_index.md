Hi, I'm Srinidhi Ramanujam. I build production AI systems for regulated enterprises. I work on the parts that come after a promising demo: architecture, data integration, evaluation checks, controls, rollout, and user trust.

I have spent 17+ years shipping ML and AI systems, including two granted US patents from production ML work. Today my focus is enterprise GenAI for BFSI workflows, including financial analytics agents, text-to-SQL, agentic ingestion, and the evaluation and governance work that makes those systems usable.

- **[Context engineering experiments](https://github.com/srinidhi621/context-engineering-experiments):** Controlled experiments on long context, retrieval, structured prompts, latency, and answer quality.
- **[Quant Magic](https://github.com/srinidhi621/quant-magic-SandP-500):** Natural language to SQL financial analytics over public S&P 500 data.
